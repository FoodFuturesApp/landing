## Facts to get right

- Name: "Food Futures" on the website, "FoodFutures" in flyer, deck and LinkedIn. Domain foodfuturesapp.com.
- Pitch: "Make a difference, one meal at a time." Motto: "Better, not perfect."
- Stage: pre-launch. Pilot goal: 3 to 5 U.S. universities, up to 500 active users, Penn State menu first. Universities pay, students use it.
- Impact is carbon, water, land (and eutrophication) plus nutrition, never carbon alone. Data: Poore and Nemecek 2018 per 100 g. Labels: Eat often, sometimes, rarely.
- Origin: EU Horizon ATARCA (Finland), Climatebase Fellowship.
- Founder and contact: Shreya Sood. Dev and infra: Vineeth.

- Do not state numbers from the dataset as final. Several rows are empty and the label rule is undocumented.
