Privacy policy

Last updated October 9, 2026

Food Futures is pre-launch. This website shares what we are building and lets you join our waitlist.

What we collect

When you join the waitlist, a Google Form asks for your name and email address. It also asks whether you would like to test an early version, what interests you most, and how you heard about us. We collect nothing else.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitor IP addresses as part of running the service.

How we use it

We use your answers to tell you about Food Futures and to invite people to test early versions. We do not sell your information. Responses are stored in Google Forms.

Your choices

Email info@foodfuturesapp.com to see, correct or delete your information, or to leave the waitlist.
