## Posts (all organic, one about every 1 to 2 weeks)

1. About 3 days old: individual choices vs collective effect. Tragedy of the Commons (overfishing) used as the analogy, applied in reverse: many consumers repeatedly choosing more sustainable food shifts demand and norms. "Individual action is not the whole solution, but it can be an important part of how collective change begins." Tags: SustainableFood, FoodSystems, BehaviorChange, CollectiveAction, ClimateAction, ClimateTech, FoodFutures.
2. About 2 weeks old: "Does low carbon mean low impact overall?" Beef vs shrimp from the dataset. Shrimp has much lower carbon, much higher water, so it is "eat sometimes", not "eat often". FoodFutures looks across carbon, water and land plus nutrition. Data adapted from Poore and Nemecek (2018), per 100 g. Goal: make trade-offs transparent, not label foods good or bad.
3. About 4 weeks old: "What makes one food choice better than another?" Food has to nourish, fit budget, allergies, culture and habits, and increasingly show planet impact beyond carbon. Approach: **"Better, not perfect."** Ends with a question to the audience: what feels hardest to balance? Tags: FoodFutures, SustainableFood, FoodSystems, BehaviourChange, ProductDesign.

## Voice and positioning

- Plain, calm, non-judgmental. "Better, not perfect." No guilt, no good vs bad foods.
- Always more than carbon: water, land, nutrition, trade-offs.
- Cite the science (Poore and Nemecek 2018) and say the data is adapted.
- Individual action matters mostly through collective effects.
- Ends with a question to invite replies.
- Hashtags: #FoodFutures #SustainableFood #FoodSystems #BehaviourChange #ClimateTech.
