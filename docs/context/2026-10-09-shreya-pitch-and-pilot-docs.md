## Flyer
- Headline: "Empower Students to make Climate-Friendly Food Choices". Fall 2026 pilot, no cost, for early U.S. campus partners. Call to action: book a 20-minute intro call.
- Three benefits: boost student engagement by highlighting lower-impact meals, communicate impact visually at individual and campus level, be a green campus leader.
- Contact line lists info@foodfuturesapp.com and the website www.foodfutures.framer.com. That URL is wrong. Use https://www.foodfuturesapp.com.

## Deck
- Problem: uncertain impact of choices, carbon tunnel vision, lack of contextual impact data.
- Key features: location selection, home screen with a menu index, validate meal selection by photo.
- Next steps with a campus partner: connect to dining hall systems to pre-populate menu ingredients and allergens, circulate a student survey, include sign-up details (QR code) in Welcome Week materials. Footnote: data on food items and student eating habits is shared only with university leadership.
