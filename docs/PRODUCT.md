# Food Futures: product context

Status: draft. Started 2026-10-06 from the public website, extended 2026-10-09 with Shreya's pilot flyer and pitch deck (see `docs/context/2026-10-09-shreya-pitch-and-pilot-docs.md`).

## What we know (from foodfutures.framer.website)

- **Pitch:** "Make a difference, one meal at a time." A data-driven approach to a sustainable lifestyle through informed food choices.
- **Stage:** pre-launch. Every CTA is "Join Waitlist" and goes to a Google Form (https://forms.gle/pmGonK4UhYX1k7f49).
- **Beliefs on the site:**
  - Balanced eating, not strict diets: realistic choices healthier for people and planet.
  - Bigger picture: nutrition plus peer-reviewed data on water, land, biodiversity and climate, not carbon alone.
  - Better together: show impact at personal and collective levels.
- **How it works (3 steps):** View impact, validate impact, track impact. The step descriptions on the site are still template placeholder text.
- **App screens shown:**
  - Food impact card, e.g. "Soy (tofu, edamame) per 100g protein" with carbon, water, land and eutrophication, and an "Eat often" rating.
  - Planetary boundaries balanced against nutrition needs.
  - CO2e per 100g protein scale: plant-based 200g, fish 300g, chicken 380g, pork 570g, cheese 1300g, beef 1900g.
  - Meal photo capture ("Take a picture of your meal!"), then confirm or retake.
  - Lunch impact tracking over time.
- **Brand:** logo is a globe with "Food Futures" wordmark. Page title still says "Nova" (the Framer template name).

## From Shreya's flyer and pitch deck (2026-10-09)

- **What it is:** a student-facing app combining nutrition, sustainability insights and habit tracking. Tagline: "Empowering people to build low-impact food habits that support both personal and planetary wellbeing."
- **Origin:** developed through the EU Horizon ATARCA project in Finland, now advanced through the Climatebase Fellowship. Screens show Helsinki campus cafes (UniCafe).
- **Users and market:** university students. A no-cost Fall 2026 pilot is being offered to early U.S. campus partners.
- **Problem it names:** people can't tell if their food choices matter, current tools look at carbon only (not water, land, animal welfare), and impact figures ignore where food is grown, transported and packaged.
- **Screens:** choose where you eat (at home or a campus outlet), a menu with a per-meal impact index graded A to F, and "take a picture of your meal" to validate what you ate. Tabs: Lunch Menu, My Impact History, My Wallet.
- **Spelling:** the flyer and deck write "FoodFutures"; the website writes "Food Futures".

## Answered by the shared docs (2026-10-15)

One-page summary: `docs/QUICK-CONTEXT.md`. Detail in `docs/context/2026-10-15-*.md`.

- **Platform:** web app first (leaning web over app stores). The prototype is Expo and also runs in the browser.
- **Data sources:** Poore and Nemecek 2018 for impacts, USDA FNDDS 2021 to 2023 for nutrition. FWI = Food Wellbeing Index, six levels A to F, summed over carbon, water and land (see data note).
- **My Wallet:** tokens for good choices, redeemable at campus partners. Idea stage.
- **Scope and owners:** Vineeth owns web development (with Divyan) and cloud/infrastructure. Shreya owns product and UX/UI. Joshua owns the dataset, Arshiya outreach.
- **Timeline:** Q4 2026 web MVP with Penn State menu, Q1 2027 pilot with 3 to 5 universities, up to 500 users.
